      About PhishGuard AI

      How it works and what it looks for.

      How It Works

      PhishGuard AI connects to a local MCP (Model Context Protocol) server running in Node.js. When you submit an email, the AI model calls the server's analyze_email tool, which runs a battery of pattern-matching detectors against the text and returns a structured result.

      No email text is ever sent to an external server. All analysis happens on your machine.

      Phishing Indicators Detected

      Urgency Language
      Phrases designed to make you panic and act without thinking — "Act now," "Your account will be suspended," "Expires in 24 hours."
      Suspicious URLs
      Links using IP addresses, URL shorteners, or typosquatted domain names (e.g., paypa1.com instead of paypal.com).
      Credential Harvesting
      Requests for passwords, payment card numbers, social security numbers, or other sensitive data.
      Spoofed Sender
      An email that claims to be from a well-known brand (PayPal, Amazon, etc.) but links to an unrelated or suspicious domain.
      Generic Greeting
      Legitimate companies usually address you by name. "Dear Customer" is a common phishing tell.
      Threats
      Threats of legal action, fines, or arrest used to pressure you into complying.

      Email Safety Tips

      • Always hover over links before clicking to verify the real destination URL.
      • When in doubt, navigate directly to the company's website — never use links in emails.
      • Enable two-factor authentication on all important accounts.
      • Report suspected phishing emails to your email provider and IT team.
      • Never enter credentials on a page you reached by clicking an email link.
